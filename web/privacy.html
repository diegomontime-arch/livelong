  • "Personal Information" — as defined by Cal. Civ. Code §1798.140(o) (CCPA/CPRA).
  • "Educational Content" — general financial-education material that is not insurance advice.

3. Information we collect

3.1 From Agents

  • Email (used as login).
  • Display name, profile photo, WhatsApp number, bio.
  • Public URL slug.
  • State insurance license number (where collected).

3.2 From Prospects

  • Name and phone number.

4. How we use it

  • To qualify leads and route them to the Agent.
  • To enable communication between the Agent and the Prospect (WhatsApp deep link).
  • To improve the Service and detect abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations, including state insurance regulations.

5. Educational AI — "Ana"

The conversational assistant inside the Service ("Ana") provides general financial-education content. Ana does NOT recommend insurance products, give pricing, or make personalized suitability determinations. All recommendations come from a human Agent licensed in the Prospect's state. Conversations with Ana are processed by Anthropic, PBC (see §6).

9. Data retention

  • Agent account data: while the subscription is active, then 6 years for tax and audit compliance.
  • Prospect leads not converted: 24 months.
  • Prospect leads converted to a policy: retained by the licensed Agent per state insurance regulation (typically 5-7 years after policy termination).
  • Conversations with Ana: not persisted by HitLook; Anthropic retains transient logs subject to its own policy.
  • System logs: 30 days.
  • Privacy audit logs (hashed): 3 years.

10. Security measures

  • TLS 1.2+ for all network traffic.
  • Firebase Security Rules enforced server-side.
  • App Check (when enabled) verifies that callers are legitimate app instances.
  • Secrets stored in Google Secret Manager and Cloudflare Workers secrets.
  • Limited employee access on a need-to-know basis.
